The Prince Is Free!

I was not afraid, truly. The dryads fed me honey and plums, and sang through the walls at night. They are not cruel, only lonely, and rather good at planning.

I see it now. There was never any danger. They wanted a knight to come, and to come on foot, one seal at a time. I was merely the excuse.

Thou hast crossed their bridge and sat upon their bench. The squirrels know thy face. The creek hath heard thee sing, or whatever that was.

I thank thee, Knight. Go home the long way, and see what the trees have to say.
